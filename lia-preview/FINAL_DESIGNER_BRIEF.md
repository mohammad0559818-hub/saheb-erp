# Lia Cosmetics — Final Designer Handoff

## Creative Direction
Quiet Luxury + Clean Clinical Feminine.

The collection must feel premium, modern, trustworthy, feminine, and suitable for the Saudi/GCC beauty market. Avoid decorative clutter, flowers, faces, excessive gold, or packaging that resembles another brand.

## Master Brand
**Lia Cosmetics**

Recommended lockup:
- Lia: elegant serif wordmark
- Cosmetics: small uppercase sans serif
- Optional descriptor: SKIN • BEAUTY • CARE

## Master Palette
- Warm Ivory: #FFFAF6
- Dusty Rose: #C98F89
- Powder Blue: #9FC9D7
- Hydra Aqua: #9ED6D4
- Soft Sage: #BAC8B5
- Main text: #181313

## Packaging Finish
- Matte / soft-touch preferred
- Optional subtle champagne-gold hot foil only on "Lia"
- Keep printing economical for 200–500 pcs/SKU
- Use stock packaging where possible; no custom mold in first run

## SKU 01 — Hydra Glow Serum
- 30 ml
- Frosted glass dropper
- Dusty Rose accent
- Positioning: Hydrate • Brighten • Smooth
- Premium K-beauty inspired feel, but original Lia identity

## SKU 02 — Barrier Calm Moisturizer
- 50 ml
- Soft-touch tube
- White/Ivory base + Powder Blue accent
- Positioning: Sensitive Skin • Barrier Support • Daily Moisture
- Clean dermo look; do NOT imitate La Roche-Posay packaging

## SKU 03 — Cloud Hydra Mist
- 100 ml
- Fine-mist bottle
- Semi-transparent aqua bottle + white spray head
- Positioning: Refresh • Hydrate • Soothe

## SKU 04 — Daily Balance Cream
- 50–60 ml
- Matte jar
- Warm Ivory base + Soft Sage accent
- Positioning: Daily Moisture • Barrier • Comfort

## Front Label Hierarchy
1. Lia Cosmetics
2. Product name
3. Max 3 short benefits
4. Net content

## Back Label Must Reserve Space For
- Full INCI ingredients
- Directions
- Warnings
- Manufacturer
- Country of origin
- Batch / MFG / EXP
- Barcode
- Arabic + English regulatory copy

## Designer Deliverables
- Front and back label for all 4 SKUs
- Outer carton design for all 4 SKUs
- Front/back 3D mockups
- Editable AI files
- Print-ready PDF
- CMYK and Pantone references
- Dielines
- Barcode safe area
- Social media hero shot of the full collection

## Important
This document is art direction. Final claims, ingredients, warnings, and regulatory wording must be confirmed from the manufacturer and Saudi regulatory requirements before printing.
